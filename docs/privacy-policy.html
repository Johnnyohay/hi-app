Hi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Hi ("the app") is a private network app for staying in touch with the people in your school community — asking for help, reconnecting with contacts, and sharing what you can offer others. This page explains what information the app collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide (or, if you sign in with Google or Microsoft, the name, email, and profile photo shared by that provider). We restrict account creation to verified school email addresses.

Once signed in, we store the information you choose to add to your profile — your name, photo, bio, skills, current requests, and any social media links you add — along with the messages and requests you send to other members of your network.

How we use it

We use this information solely to operate the app: to authenticate you, to show your profile to other members of your network, to match your requests with people who can help, and to deliver messages between members.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties except the identity providers you choose to sign in with (Google, Microsoft) and the infrastructure providers that host the app's backend.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Hi is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the project's GitHub issues page.
