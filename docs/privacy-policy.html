Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

This page explains what Hi collects, how it's used, who else can see it, and what outside services it passes through. This is a working, plain-language policy for the product as it actually operates today — not a template.

Information we collect

When you create an account: the email and password you provide.

When you build your profile, only what you choose to add: name, role, your current city and, optionally, the city you're originally from, bio, the skills you list, what you're currently looking for, links to your other profiles, and an optional profile photo. Location is only ever collected as a map coordinate you either type in (via city search) or share from your device — never tracked in the background, and never required.

Content you create: messages you send in a one-to-one chat, requests ("asks") you share alongside your profile, and ratings you give other members. There's no public feed — just these two things and your profile.

Who can see what

Hi is a directory, not a private diary — your name, role, city, bio, skills, and what you offer are visible to every other signed-in member; that visibility is how matching works. The same is true for the requests you share.

Messages are different: only you and the other person in a conversation can read it. Nobody else, including other members, can see your messages.

Ratings you give or receive are visible only to the two people involved — not shown publicly and not visible to the rest of the network.

How we use it

Solely to run the app: authenticating you, showing your profile to other members, matching requests with people who might help, delivering messages, and showing you feedback you've received. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Services we rely on

Hi's backend — accounts, database, file storage — runs on Supabase, which processes this data on our behalf as our infrastructure provider. When you search for a city, that search text is sent to Mapbox to look up matching places. If the app crashes or hits an error, technical details about that error (not your messages or profile content) are sent to Sentry so we can fix it. We don't share your information with anyone beyond what's needed to operate the app through these providers.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete or edit most of your profile directly in the app at any time. To delete your account and all associated data entirely and immediately, go to Profile → Account → Delete account in the app.

Security

Passwords must meet a minimum strength requirement and are never stored in plain text. On mobile, your session is encrypted using your device's own secure storage rather than plain app storage. Access to every table in our database is restricted at the database level — not just in the app's own code — so, for example, only you and a conversation's other participant can ever read that conversation's messages.

Children's privacy

Hi is an adults-only service: you must be 18 or older to use it, and it is not directed at anyone younger. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent via github.com/Johnnyohay/hi-app/issues.
